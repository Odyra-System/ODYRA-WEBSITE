## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by `build.py` (Python standard library), GSAP for motion, served by nginx on Railway. Chosen by the user over Astro and Next.js because every word must be in the served HTML for search engines and AI crawlers that do not run JavaScript.

## Users

Primary: owners, CEOs and product leads of software houses that sell management software (gestionali) to small businesses: salons, dental studios, garages, hotels, veterinary clinics. They evaluate whether to offer an AI agent to their own customers under their own brand. Secondary: companies with large contact volumes looking for custom sales and customer-service agents.

## Product Purpose

Odyra System builds vertical AI agents that answer the phone and WhatsApp, book, move and cancel appointments following the rules of one specific trade. The agents connect to management software, which resells them white-label to its customers. Success for the site: a software house understands the model in seconds, believes Odyra is technically ahead, and requests a demo.

## Positioning

Sector rules written in code, not only in a prompt, plus a dedicated connector that reads and writes inside the management software. Delivered white-label and operated by Odyra (quality, errors, consumption, handoff to a person). Proven in production in beauty with GoAgent for BOSS / GoWeb (1,500+ salons, since June 2026).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, events (BOSS convention, October 2026), search and AI assistants. Decision is B2B and partnership-driven; the action is a demo request through the contact form or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Channels: real-time voice and WhatsApp Business (Odyra is Meta Tech Provider via Vonage; the account stays owned by the customer).
- Five platform layers: sector rules, connectors, channels, operations, compliance (GDPR, AI Act; the agent always says it is an AI).
- New customer activation: staff and price list sync nightly; channels set up in about ten minutes.
- Other products: HAKO (condominium concierge software, hakocondomini.com); custom projects (e.g. Gruppo Colzani).
- Bilingual site: Italian at root, Spanish under /es/.
- Every page keeps its Markdown twin, JSON-LD, llms.txt and sitemap; no content may depend on JavaScript.
