## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by `build.py` (Python standard library), GSAP for motion, served by nginx on Railway. Chosen by the user over Astro and Next.js because every word must be in the served HTML for search engines and AI crawlers that do not run JavaScript.

## Users

Odyra is a white-label AI agent factory. Four buyer groups, all addressed by the home page (confirmed October 2026):
- Software houses (management software, vertical software) that want to offer AI agents to their customers under their own brand.
- Large private companies with high call and contact volumes: sales, customer service, back office.
- Franchising and retail networks: one consistent agent for the whole network, managed centrally.
- Agencies and system integrators that resell technology and want an AI engine to rebrand.
The person evaluating is a decision maker (owner, CEO, CTO, head of operations or sales) asking: is this supplier credible, can I put my name on it, who runs it.

## Product Purpose

In one sentence (confirmed): Odyra is a factory of white-label AI agents; the client puts its name on them, Odyra provides and runs the engine. Odyra System builds vertical AI agents that answer the phone and WhatsApp, book, move and cancel appointments following the rules of one specific trade. The agents connect to management software, which resells them white-label to its customers. Success for the site: a decision maker from any of the four buyer groups understands the model in seconds, believes Odyra is a credible, technically advanced supplier, and requests a demo.

## Positioning

Sector rules written in code, not only in a prompt, plus a dedicated connector that reads and writes inside the management software. Delivered white-label and operated by Odyra (quality, errors, consumption, handoff to a person). Proven in production with BOSS: GoAgent ("GoWeb Agent") is the white-label agent built only for BOSS and its GoWeb salons (1,500+ salons, since June 2026). GoAgent is a client case, BOSS's product, never presented as an Odyra product or as the site's centre.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, events (BOSS convention, October 2026), search and AI assistants. Decision is B2B and partnership-driven; the action is a demo request through the contact form or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Channels: real-time voice and WhatsApp Business (Odyra is Meta Tech Provider via Vonage; the account stays owned by the customer).
- Five platform layers: sector rules, connectors, channels, operations, compliance (GDPR, AI Act; the agent always says it is an AI).
- New customer activation: staff and price list sync nightly; channels set up in about ten minutes.
- Other products: HAKO (condominium concierge software, hakocondomini.com); custom projects (e.g. Gruppo Colzani).
- Bilingual site: Italian at root, Spanish under /es/.
- Every page keeps its Markdown twin, JSON-LD, llms.txt and sitemap; no content may depend on JavaScript.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Odyra System. Company: Verypos S.r.l., Viale Papiniano 8, 20123 Milano, P.IVA 11630390968.
- Logo stays: circuit mark in cyan on deep navy. Site colours must stay close to the logo: deep navy and cyan family (user decision, October 2026, after rejecting a green-and-gold preview as too far from the logo).
- Voice: plain, precise, no hype claims; facts over adjectives.
- Desired effect: "wow". The company must feel ahead of everyone.

## Product Principles

1. The client's brand comes first; Odyra is the engine behind it, and must still look like a credible, solid supplier.
2. Show the agent working, do not describe it.
3. Sector rules in code are the differentiator: make them visible.
4. Speak to all four buyer groups without diluting the message.
5. Readable by people and machines alike.
